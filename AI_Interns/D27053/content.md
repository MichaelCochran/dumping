Lockheed Martin's internal AI ecosystem — AI Factory, Genesis, Panel, Gond, LMADK, the MCP Blueprint, and multiple approved coding assistants — is powerful but scattered across separate, fast-changing docs. Developers struggle to find the right approved tool, assemble a correct end-to-end path, and meet security and data compliance obligations (e.g., the new MCP Blueprint), and often build duplicate solutions because they didn't know an alternative existed. Making matters worse, today's AI tools have no knowledge of LM's internal AI technology, so asking them about it produces hallucinated answers.

The AI Development Agent addresses this by acting as a single, LM-aware expert that consolidates this knowledge, routes developers to the right platform, and bakes in security from the start — so they build AI, ML, agent, and MCP solutions faster and correctly on approved platforms.
