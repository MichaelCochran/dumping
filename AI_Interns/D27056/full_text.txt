Solution Overview*

Pathfinder... the Solution

- An AI-powered career development chatbot for Lockheed Martin employees

- Will provide personalized career recommendations and identify skill gaps

Target Audience:

Lockheed Martin Employees: All levels (entry, mid-career, senior) and disciplines (technical, business, HR).

Managers/Mentors: Those who guide and support employee career development.

HR/Talent Development: Responsible for designing/implementing career development programs.

﻿﻿Personalized Recommendations
    Suggests career paths, training, and development opportunities based on employee profiles and goals.
Career Path Analysis
    Provides insights into required skills and steps for desired career paths.
Development Roadmaps
    Generates customized plans with recommended actions and timelines.
Progress Tracking
    Logs completed activities and updates development plans.
Chat-Based Interface
    Offers conversational support for quick queries and guidance.
Integration with Existing Tools
    Can access employee data through resume, Atlas profile downloads, or even conversation.

Value Proposition*

Improved Career Satisfaction: Enhanced employee engagement and fulfillment in their careers.

    Increased Efficiency: Streamlined career development processes.

    Enhanced Skill Development: Employees acquire new skills relevant to their career goals.

﻿


- Employees who are engaged and have clear career goals are 2.5 times more likely to thrive in their careers.

- AI-powered platforms can reduce the time spent on career planning

- Personalized experiences can significantly enhance employee engagement and satisfaction

﻿

Benefits

    Cost Savings: Reduced turnover costs and operational expenses.

    Efficiency Improvements: Employees looking for other opportunities like stretch assignments do so with a clear goal in mind and specific skills to develop.

Data Sources: LM Intranet sources, including procedures and policies for career development
