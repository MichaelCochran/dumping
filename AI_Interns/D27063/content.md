An AI agent + Dashboard built in to Navigator pulling in context from MS Teams and Outlook
Based on a survey our team created and distributed to 140 Lockheed Martin employees across every business area, we found that employees repeatedly lose productive time rebuilding context from Outlook, Teams, calendars, and browser-based tools. The problem is especially severe at two points: the start of each workday and the return from extended time away. Respondents reported spending an average of 78 minutes each morning reviewing messages, meetings, and updates before beginning focused work, while employees returning after more than a week away reported a median catch-up time of 3.5 hours.

Because important decisions, requests, action items, and deadlines are spread across multiple systems, employees must manually determine what changed, what matters, and what requires action. This creates unnecessary context switching, repeated reading, missed information, and delayed work. UNIFY addresses this challenge by bringing those scattered signals together and transforming them into a concise, prioritized brief that helps employees start ready and return caught up.

AI-powered Navigator agent: UNIFY is a browser-extension prototype that gathers work context and sends it to LM Navigator for summarization and prioritization.

Morning Brief: Creates a clear start-of-day summary of important emails, Teams messages, meetings, priorities, and action items.

Out-of-Office Catch-Up: Condenses missed updates, decisions, requests, deadlines, and follow-ups into one organized return brief.

Action Item Detection: Identifies tasks, unanswered requests, and commitments that may be buried in long email threads or Teams conversations.

Multi-source context collection: Pulls relevant information from Outlook, Teams, Calendar, and selected browser tabs so employees do not have to review each source separately.

Priority-based organization: Sorts information by importance and required action rather than simply presenting it chronologically.

Source traceability: Links summarized items back to their original source so users can verify the information and quickly open the relevant message or page.

Secure, read-only workflow: The prototype summarizes and organizes existing information without autonomously sending messages, deleting content, or performing consequential actions.
Value Proposition*
UNIFY's value proposition is straightforward: it gives employees back time currently spent reconstructing work context. In our team's survey of 140 Lockheed Martin employees across every business area, respondents reported spending an average of 78 minutes each morning reviewing Teams, Outlook, and Calendar, while employees returning from more than a week away reported a median catch-up time of 3.5 hours. Using a conservative 30% reduction in morning review time, UNIFY could recover about 27 minutes per employee per day, or roughly 90 hours per employee each year. For out-of-office recovery, a 50% reduction would return approximately 105 minutes each time an employee comes back from extended leave.

At enterprise scale, those minutes translate into substantial mission capacity. A deployment to 1,000 active users could recover approximately 90,000 productive hours annually, representing about $9 million in capacity at a $100 fully loaded hourly labor rate. At 10,000 users, that increases to roughly 900,000 hours and $90 million in annual capacity value. Beyond direct time savings, UNIFY reduces context switching, repeated reading, and the risk of missing important requests or decisions by giving employees a prioritized, source-linked view of what changed and what requires attention.
