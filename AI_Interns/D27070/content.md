Lockheed Martin offers a wealth of information about career pathways and development programs.​

The sheer volume of information is not only overwhelming, but also scattered across many websites, portals, and document libraries.​

Employees often struggle to locate the roles that align with their interest or know what skills they need to build to reach their career goal. There is simply no direction.​

Many employee's feel uneasy about asking about other roles with their managers, fearing it might be perceived as a lack of commitment to their current job.
