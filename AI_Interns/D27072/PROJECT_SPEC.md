## 1. Problem Statement

Hire Coach addresses the challenges organizations face during periods of high-volume hiring by helping hiring managers conduct structured, consistent, and efficient interviews. Many hiring managers, especially those who are new to interviewing or need a refresher, spend significant time reviewing resumes and creating role-specific interview questions. This often leads to inconsistent interview quality, varying candidate evaluations, and longer hiring timelines. Another challenge is that qualified candidates who are not selected for one position are frequently overlooked for other opportunities within the organization, forcing recruiters to spend additional time sourcing new applicants. Hire Coach uses AI to analyze resumes, generate personalized STAR-based interview questions, monitor for potentially biased or legally inappropriate questions, and capture candidate skills and qualifications. After each interview, the AI prompts hiring managers with reflective questions to identify candidates who may be a better fit for other roles, allowing sourcing teams to reconnect with qualified talent instead of losing them from the hiring pipeline. By improving interview consistency, reducing preparation time, supporting internal applicants with personalized interview preparation, and strengthening collaboration between hiring managers and sourcing teams, Hire Coach enables organizations to hire more efficiently while maximizing the value of their existing talent pool.

---

## 2. Solution Overview

Hire Coach is an AI-powered interview assistant designed to support hiring managers, sourcing teams, and internal job applicants throughout the hiring process. The AI analyzes candidate resumes to generate personalized, role-specific interview questions using the STAR interview methodology, helping hiring managers conduct structured and consistent interviews while reducing preparation time.

Key features include AI-powered resume parsing and skill extraction, dynamic interview question generation, compliance monitoring that flags potentially discriminatory or legally inappropriate questions, and automated tagging of candidate qualifications such as skills, education, certifications, and experience.

Following each interview, Hire Coach asks reflective questions to help identify candidates who may be qualified for other open positions. This information is shared with sourcing teams, enabling organizations to retain qualified talent and improve candidate redeployment. For internal applicants, the AI generates personalized interview questions based on the position they are pursuing, allowing employees to better prepare for interviews. Together, these capabilities improve hiring efficiency, enhance interview consistency, and strengthen the organization's talent pipeline.

---

## 3. Value Proposition

Hire Coach delivers measurable business value by improving the speed, consistency, and effectiveness of the hiring process. By automatically generating personalized STAR-based interview questions and analyzing resumes, the AI reduces interview preparation time and helps hiring managers make more informed hiring decisions. This enables recruiters and hiring managers to fill positions more quickly during periods of high-volume hiring.

The solution also strengthens Lockheed Martin's talent pipeline by identifying qualified candidates who may be a better fit for other open positions, reducing the need to source new applicants and maximizing the value of existing recruiting efforts. For internal employees, Hire Coach generates personalized interview questions that help them prepare for career advancement opportunities, supporting internal mobility and employee development.

Additionally, the AI promotes standardized interviewing practices by flagging potentially biased or legally inappropriate questions, reducing compliance risks and improving consistency across hiring teams. Overall, Hire Coach increases recruiter productivity, reduces recruiting costs, improves the candidate experience, and helps Lockheed Martin attract, evaluate, and retain top talent more efficiently.
