## 1. Problem Statement

Current training at Lockheed Martin relies heavily on static work instructions that are often outdated, overly technical, and difficult for new hires to interpret without extensive tribal knowledge. These documents can contain ambiguous steps, undefined jargon, and assumptions about prior experience, which slows onboarding, increases dependence on subject-matter experts, and creates variability in how tasks are performed. ﻿My AI agent addresses this by transforming traditional work instructions into an interactive, guided learning experience. Instead of expecting new hires to "decode" complex documentation, the agent walks them through each procedure step by step, explains the intent behind each action, defines unfamiliar terminology in context, and answers questions in real time. It leverages existing work-instruction files stored in a database, parses them, and presents the content in clearer, task-focused language tailored to the user's knowledge level. ﻿This reduces training friction, improves comprehension and consistency, and helps bridge the gap between formal documentation and practical know-how. Ultimately, the agent aims to accelerate time-to-competency for new employees while improving adherence to standardized processes and reducing the burden on experienced staff who currently must interpret and explain instructions manually.

---

## 2. Solution Overview

My AI agent prototype converts existing work-instruction documents into an interactive training experience for new hires. It connects to a database of work instructions, ingests a selected file, and automatically breaks the procedure into clear, sequential steps. For each step, the agent explains what to do, why it matters, and how it relates to upstream and downstream tasks.

The key features include:

- Step-by-step guidance

Presents one actionable step at a time, reducing cognitive overload and helping users focus on the current task.

- Contextual explanations

Translates technical jargon into plain language, defines acronyms, and provides background context so new hires can understand rather than just follow instructions.

- Interactive Q&A

Allows users to ask questions about any step and receive tailored explanations, examples, or clarifications in real time.

- Adaptive support

Adjusts the level of detail based on the user's familiarity with the task, offering more basic explanations for beginners and deeper nuance for more advanced users.

By layering an intelligent conversational interface on top of existing documentation, the agent helps standardize training, reduce misunderstandings, and accelerate time-to-competency without requiring a complete rewrite of all work instructions.

---

## 3. Value Proposition

My AI agent strengthens Lockheed Martin's operations by transforming how procedural knowledge is taught and applied on the shop floor and in office workflows. ﻿First, it reduces onboarding time by giving new hires an interactive coach that explains tasks, clarifies jargon, and answers questions in real time, instead of relying solely on static documents and limited mentor availability. A faster time to proficiency lowers training costs, helps critical programs staff up more quickly, and reduces the need for new hires to pull experienced employees away from their core work for one-on-one training. ﻿Second, it improves process consistency and quality. By guiding users' step by step and reinforcing standardized procedures, the agent helps reduce variation in how tasks are performed, which can lower rework, defect rates, and schedule risk, particularly in complex, highly regulated environments. ﻿Third, it captures and scales tribal knowledge. As employees ask questions and the agent surfaces clarifications, that insight can be fed back into the knowledge base, gradually enhancing documentation and institutional memory across sites and programs. ﻿Finally, the agent frees up experienced personnel from repetitive training and clarification duties, allowing them to focus more on high-value work and innovation, while the AI handles routine guidance and explanation.
