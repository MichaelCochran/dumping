## 1. Problem Statement

Requirements development is an iterative and lengthy process. Countless hours can be spent pouring over notes, audio recordings, and email threads to extract, validate, and formalize stakeholder intent. ﻿Manual transcription leads to missed details and inconsistent "shall" statements.​ ﻿Ambiguous or incomplete requirements cause re‑work and defects.​ ﻿Traceability and verification (D‑T‑A) are often fragmented or absent. Our AI agent works to prevent all of these difficulties and improve the efficiency of Systems Engineering at Lockheed.

---

## 2. Solution Overview

**- Target audience** – System architects, program managers, requirement engineers, and cross‑functional workshop facilitators​

**- Core workflow​**

- Upload a transcript, audio, video, or structured metadata.​
- Automatic transcription.​
- AI parsing extracts functional / non‑functional requirements, risks, assumptions, and constraints.​
- Classification → type, verification method, acceptance criteria.​
- Output in selected format​
**- Key features​**

- "Shall" statement generator with template‑compliant phrasing.​
- Risk & ambiguity flagging with auto‑generated clarifying questions.​
- Verified verification‑method tagging.​
- Configurable output.​
- **Value addition** – reduces manual effort, improves consistency, surfaces hidden risks, and delivers results in minutes.​

---

## 3. Value Proposition

- **70 % faster** turnaround from meeting to complete requirements table (e.g., 30 min meeting → 9 min draft).​
- **Consistent "shall" statements **and verification codes → > 95 % compliance with RE templates, reducing re‑work.​
- **Automatic risk & ambiguity flags **capture every uncertain item early, cutting downstream change‑order costs.​
- **$12 k+ saved per quarter** per team from labor‑hour reduction and avoided risk mitigation.​
- **All processing stays on‑premise** → meets security/compliance without extra controls.
