Lockheed Martin's current LMRecognition experience relies on an AwardCo bot that provides only static, generic suggestions, making the recognition process feel time‑consuming and unrelated to day‑to‑day work. As a result, many employees skip giving recognitions, leading to low platform utilization and missed chances to reinforce the company's Core Values, Cultural Philosophies, and A4 pillars. This weak linkage also deprives leadership of the data needed to monitor and improve cultural health across the organization.

Solution Overview*

The solution is an AI‑driven LMRecognition Assistant that instantly transforms a user‑provided behavior description into three ready‑to‑post, multi‑sentence comments, each aligned with Lockheed Martin's Core Values, Cultural Philosophies, and A4 pillars. The assistant extracts name, project, and action details, inserts the appropriate attributes (up to three per comment), and attaches a concise micro‑learning nugget. Integrated with the existing AwardCo API, it also is ready to update badge progress, tracks streaks, and feeds anonymized usage data to leadership dashboards if the agent were to be integrated with the LMRecognition platform. This streamlined workflow turns recognition into a fast, personalized, and culturally reinforcing habit across the enterprise.

Value Proposition*

By delivering instant, culturally‑aligned recognitions, the AI assistant boosts employee participation in LMRecognition, leading to stronger reinforcement of core values and faster adoption of desired behaviors. Higher recognition rates translate into improved teamwork, reduced rework, and clearer data for leaders to drive continuous‑improvement—ultimately enhancing productivity and cost efficiency across Lockheed Martin operations.
