## 1. Problem Statement

Lockheed Martin offers a wealth of information about career pathways and development programs.​ The sheer volume of information is not only overwhelming, but also scattered across many websites, portals, and document libraries.​ Employees often struggle to locate the roles that align with their interest or know what skills they need to build to reach their career goal. There is simply no direction.​ Many employee's feel uneasy about asking about other roles with their managers, fearing it might be perceived as a lack of commitment to their current job.

## 3. Value Proposition

Accelerated Internal Mobility​

Reduce average time‑to‑internal‑hire from 90 days to 70 days (‑23%)​

Skill Gap Reduction​

Employees close 80% of identified gaps within 6 months via targeted training suggestions.

Improved Role Fit​

Match‑rate of recommended postings drastically increases.

Cost Saving​

Lower external recruiting spend by ≈ $1.2 M annually (fewer external hires, higher internal fill rate). Saved recruiter fees, advertising spend, and onboarding time. (Approximation)

Efficiency​

Efficiency: Faster matchmaking frees recruiters for strategic work.​

Quality​

Higher‑fit placements reduce turnover risk and increase productivity.
