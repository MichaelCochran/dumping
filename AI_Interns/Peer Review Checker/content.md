The peer‑review process at Lockheed Martin often requires many engineers to spend valuable time correcting grammatical and compliance errors in submitted documents. These language‑level issues detract from the engineers' primary focus on technical content and can delay the overall review cycle. There is currently no automated, easy‑to‑use pre‑check tool that allows authors to simply upload their Word document and receive a comprehensive report of grammar and compliance problems before the formal review begins.

This agent is designed to help all engineers, but especially high-level engineers, review documents more efficiently. 

These documents often need to follow specific templates and standards. The agent can read a document and provide targeted feedback on grammatical, structural, and content-related issues. 

It quotes the exact lines from the original document and clearly states the specific changes that should be made, helping ensure consistency, clarity, and compliance with required formats.
Value Proposition*
**Accelerates peer‑review cycles** – By catching language and compliance issues up‑front, reviewers spend less time on non‑technical edits and can move to technical evaluation more quickly.

**Reduces document defects** – A pre‑check improves the first‑pass quality of submissions, lowering the number of re‑work iterations needed before final approval.

**Ensures a professional final document** – Consistent grammar, correct terminology, and adherence to policy standards present a polished, credible document to senior engineers and customers.

**Boosts overall efficiency** – Engineers devote minimal effort to language fixes, freeing their time for high‑impact design and analysis work.

**Delivers higher‑quality products for approval** – Cleaner, compliant documents increase confidence among high‑level engineers, facilitating faster and more reliable approval decisions.
Additional Information
We would like to treat the current prototype as an MVP and add incremental enhancements. One improvement is an ELM widget that lets users simply drag‑and‑drop a Word document into the peer‑review tool, keeping the entire workflow within a single interface. Another improvement is refining the agent so it can generate an "approved‑changes" Word file that automatically applies the user‑approved edits while preserving the original layout, styling, and embedded objects, further streamlining the review process.
