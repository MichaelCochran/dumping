Solution Overview*

To address the long downtimes and delays in changes, we developed Policy Flow AI, which streamlines the policy update process by analyzing meeting transcripts, identifying proposed policy changes, and generating an updated policy document for the user. Leveraging AI assistance, our solution reduces manual effort, preserves meeting context, and accelerates the process. This creates a centralized source that enables users to efficiently make changes to documents without manual intervention.

Value Proposition*

The Collaborative Policy/Document Writing Streamliner modernizes the policy development process by transforming meeting discussions into review-ready policy drafts. By leveraging AI, meeting transcripts, and existing documentation, the solution reduces manual effort, shortens document turnaround, improves consistency, and empowers subject matter experts to focus on decision-making instead of administrative work. The result is a faster, more standardized, and scalable documentation process that maintains human oversight and organizational governance.
