In Tech Pubs, writers frequently need to confirm the correct style, format, or grammar while marking up manuals. 

The official references for these decisions are the OMMS Style Guide (SMP 3233, ~112 pages) and the Writer's Guide (SMP 1771, ~64 pages), both provided only as PDF documents.

The challenge, therefore, is to reduce the time and effort required for Tech Pubs writers to locate and understand the specific rules in SMP 3233 and SMP 1771 that apply to their current markup task.

The assistant:

Retrieves relevant sections from the OMMS Style Guide (SMP 3233) and the Writer's Guide (SMP 1771),

Value Proposition*
